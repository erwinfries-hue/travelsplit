# TravelSplit – Handbook

## Everyone

**Join:** open the invitation link → sign in with Google (recommended) or as a guest. A guest account lives in this
browser only; if it is lost, the trip admin has to send a new link. Under **Profile** a guest can connect Google at any time.

**Add an expense:** in the trip cockpit tap "＋ Add expense". Amount, currency, title, who takes part. You are always
the payer. Optional category, note and a receipt photo (resized, location data removed).

**Quick entry:** type one line in the cockpit, e.g. `Taxi 20 EUR all`, `Dinner 85 EUR Erwin Larissa`,
`Hotel 200 EUR 50% Erwin 50% Larissa`, `#LIS26 Taxi 12 EUR all`. "Next" opens the normal form prefilled; nothing is
saved before you confirm it there. The payer is never taken from the text.

**Share from WhatsApp (Android):** long-press a message → Share → TravelSplit, then as quick entry. iPhone: copy the
text and paste it into quick entry.

**Offline:** the trip opens from the last copy (marked as such). New expenses stay on the phone as "Not sent" and only
count once they were sent online.

**Balances and repayments:** **Balances** shows who pays whom. After paying, "Report repayment"; it counts once the
receiver confirms it. Confirmed repayments never change with later rate changes.

## Trip admin

People and invitation links, approving joins of people who already have bookings, one rate per currency, booking for
someone else (marked as reconstruction), trip settings (dates, trip code, hand over admin, close, reopen, delete a
closed trip after typing its name), CSV export/import and the PDF report.
